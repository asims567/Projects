# Case Study Project
This project involves analyzing the relationship between study hours and exam scores with a random sample to determine whether they are correlated. It involves a series of testing methods and a scatter plot to showcase the trends. 

## Pearson and Correlation Test
Pearson correlation was used to evaluate the strength and direction of the linear relationship between study hours and exam scores.

<img width="295" height="242" alt="Pearson and Correlation Plot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3e66cf09-9121-4b76-9c97-ea03d1533b4b" />

## ANOVA test
A one-way ANOVA was conducted to compare exam scores across low, medium, and high study hour groups, determining whether at least one group differs significantly from the others.

<img width="295" height="242" alt="study_plot_anova" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/df829dcb-303f-4572-8546-0d022cc40935" />

## t-test
An independent t-test was used to compare exam scores between students who studied less than 5 hours and those who studied 5 or more hours, assessing whether there is a significant difference between the two groups.

<img width="317" height="242" alt="Boxplot Study Hours vs Exam Scores" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1de7862c-5daf-4b44-842f-64610c423cad" />


## Chi-Square Test
A Chi-square test was performed to examine the relationship between study group (low vs high) and exam outcome (pass/fail), assessing whether these variables are associated.
